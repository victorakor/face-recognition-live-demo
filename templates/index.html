How it works

  1. Capture — your browser grabs a frame from the webcam and encodes it as JPEG. The camera stream itself never leaves your device as video.
  2. Detect — custom-trained YOLOv8 weights locate every face and classify its covering (no_mask, mask, other_coverings), and flag any weapon in frame. If the YOLO runtime is unavailable the server falls back to dlib's HOG face detector.
  3. Describe — dlib's 68-point landmark predictor aligns each face, and the ResNet encoder maps it to a 128-dimension embedding.
  4. Match — the embedding is compared by euclidean distance against the enrolled gallery. Under the threshold it is a match; over it, the face is Unknown.

Everything runs on CPU on a free-tier container, so expect a couple of frames per second rather than real-time video. Boxes fade while the next result is in flight.
